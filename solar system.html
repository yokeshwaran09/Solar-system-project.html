Solar System

🌌 Explore the Solar System

The Solar System is a vast collection of planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and other celestial objects held together by the gravity of the Sun. At its center is the Sun, a massive star that provides the light and energy needed for life on Earth. The Solar System contains eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Each planet is unique in its size, atmosphere, temperature, and composition. Explore the planets and discover the amazing wonders of our cosmic neighborhood.

Mercury

🔴 Mercury — Mercury is the smallest and closest planet to the Sun. It is a rocky planet with a dark, cratered surface and has almost no atmosphere. Mercury experiences extreme temperatures, becoming very hot during the day and very cold at night. It completes one orbit around the Sun in about 88 Earth days.

Venus

🟠 Venus- Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is similar in size to Earth. It has a thick atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide, which creates an intense greenhouse effect. Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System, with a surface hot enough to melt lead.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. It has liquid water, a protective atmosphere, and a suitable range of temperatures for living organisms. Earth is a rocky planet with oceans, continents, mountains, forests, and diverse ecosystems.

Jupiter

🟠 Jupiter — Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the Solar System. It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter is famous for its colorful cloud bands, powerful storms, and the Great Red Spot, a huge storm in its atmosphere.

Neptune

🔵 Neptune — Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant with a deep blue appearance caused partly by methane in its atmosphere. Neptune is known for its extremely fast winds, powerful storms, and very cold temperatures.

Sun
